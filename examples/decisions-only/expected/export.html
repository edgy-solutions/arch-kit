ADR-0001 Lockers accept parcels offline

Commit SHA, DATE: decisions/0001-lockers-accept-parcels-offline.md

Front page

Status

Accepted.

1. Decision

A locker takes in a parcel and gives the sender a receipt without a link to the depot. The locker keeps the parcel's record until the link returns, then sends it on.

2. Consequences

The depot learns of a parcel later than the sender does. A receipt is not proof that the depot has the record.

ADR-0002 The depot routes parcels by size

Commit SHA, DATE: decisions/0002-depot-routes-parcels-by-size.md

Front page

Status

Accepted.

1. Decision

The depot sends a parcel to a van by its size class. Large parcels go to the cargo van and small ones to the courier bike. A parcel a locker took in offline is routed the same way once its record arrives, as ADR-0001 §2 describes.

2. Open question

How the depot routes a parcel whose size class is missing is not decided.

Status: open OF-1 — A parcel with no size class

Until it is decided, the depot holds such a parcel at the bay.
