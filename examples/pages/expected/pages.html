What Woad is

Front page

Woad runs parcel lockers that keep working when the depot is out of reach.

The idea is settled in ADR-0001, and the depot's half in other:ADR-0007. The locker side is CO-001 Built; the depot side is CO-002, which has been CO-002 Accepted.
