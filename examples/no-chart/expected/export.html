ADR-0001 Lockers accept parcels offline

Commit SHA, DATE: decisions/ADR-0001-lockers-accept-parcels-offline.md

Front page

Status

Accepted.

1. Decision

A locker takes in a parcel and gives the sender a receipt without a link to the depot. The locker keeps the parcel's record until the link returns, then sends it on.

2. Offline behaviour

With the link down, the locker still accepts parcels until its bays are full. It refuses the next one and says why.

3. Consequences

The depot learns of a parcel later than the sender does. A receipt is not proof that the depot has the record.

The first locker built to this decision was Pylon-12, and its receipts were the model for the rest.

ADR-0002 The depot routes parcels by size

Commit SHA, DATE: decisions/ADR-0002-depot-routes-parcels-by-size.md

Front page

Status

Proposed.

1. Decision

The depot sends a parcel to a van by its size class. Large parcels go to the cargo van and small ones to the courier bike.

2. Open question

How the depot routes a parcel whose size class is missing is not decided.

Status: open OF-1 — A parcel with no size class

Until it is decided, the depot holds such a parcel at the bay.
